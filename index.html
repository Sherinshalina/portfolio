I'm Shalina Sherin, a digital marketing professional focused on SEO, online visibility, and video content. I enjoy understanding how people search, shaping useful website content, and helping brands explain what makes them valuable.

My work spans keyword research, on-page and technical SEO, website audits, content planning, digital campaigns, and video presenting. That combination lets me think about both sides of a brand's online presence: how people discover it and how they connect with it.

This portfolio is a growing collection of my work, experiments, and projects. I aim to keep learning, test ideas thoughtfully, and focus on work that serves a real business goal.
